Puffy Closet

Ten fluffy puffs,
one magical closet

A cozy dress-up game for little ones. Pick a puff, open the closet, dress it up, draw your own clothes, take sweet photos and put on a runway show.

Tap, dress, strut, smile

Big friendly buttons, no reading needed. Everything fits on one screen.

Made for little hands

Simple taps, happy sounds and lots to discover.

A real closet

Hats, tees, dresses, hoodies, all kinds of glasses, necklaces and a shoe rack. Tap an item and it flies onto your puff. Just like real life, each item can be worn by one puff at a time.

Drawing studio

Paint right on your puff with crayons, brushes, a paint bucket and an eraser. Your creations go into the closet.

Photo studio

Beach, snowy hill, castle, space, garden or stage. Or paint your own background with stickers.

Puffs that move

They stand up, dance and wiggle when you tap them, so you can see their shoes up close.

Runway show

Dressed-up puffs strut onto the stage, pose under the spotlights and take a bow together while the crowd cheers.

Soft sounds

A music-box lullaby, a bouncy runway tune and gentle effects. Music and sound effects each have their own off switch.

Big and roomy on iPad

On iPad the puff sits big on the floor next to a full-height closet. On iPhone Duo the room spreads across both halves of the screen, and the puff even dances on a little runway in tent mode.

New: the big screen on Apple TV

Bring the whole family to the couch. Pick a puff with the Siri Remote, dress it up from a closet full of tabs and watch the runway show on the TV. The same app works on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV with one purchase.

Meet the puffs

Teddy, Bunbun, Mochi, Pandy, Ribbit, Puffy, Rexy, Coco and the new friends Hamham and Fifi.
