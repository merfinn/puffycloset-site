Puffy Closet

Eight fluffy puffs,
one magical closet

A cozy dress-up game for little ones. Pick a puff, open the closet, dress it up, draw your own clothes and take sweet photos.

iPhone iPad iPhone Duo
Get it on the App Store

Tap, dress, draw, smile

Big friendly buttons, no reading needed. Everything fits on one screen.

Made for little hands

Simple taps, happy sounds and lots to discover.

A real closet

Hats, tees, dresses, glasses, necklaces and a shoe rack. Tap an item and it flies onto your puff.

Drawing studio

Paint right on your puff with crayons, brushes, a paint bucket and an eraser. Your creations go into the closet.

Photo studio

Beach, snowy hill, castle, space, garden or stage. Or paint your own background with stickers.

Puffs that move

They stand up, dance and wiggle when you tap them, so you can see their shoes up close.

One owner per item

Just like real life, a sweater worn by one puff can't be worn by another. Sharing is a little lesson.

Soft sounds

A music-box lullaby and gentle effects, with a mute button for grown-ups.

Big and roomy on iPad

On iPad the puff sits big on the floor next to a full-height closet. On iPhone Duo the room spreads across both halves of the screen, and the puff even dances on a little runway in tent mode.

Meet the puffs

Teddy, Bunbun, Mochi, Pandy, Ribbit, Puffy, Rexy and Coco.
